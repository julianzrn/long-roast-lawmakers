# Candidates — The Long Roast: Lawmakers

Competition: `u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7`

Requirement (from the official requirements page): A single line of at most 320 characters roasting lawmakers.

8 candidates were drafted; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1 ← SELECTED

They wrote a law requiring every algorithm to explain itself in plain English, then filed it as a photograph of a printout, slightly crooked, in a folder named FINAL_v7_REAL.pdf.docx.

(183 characters)

## Candidate 2

They banned the word 'synergy' in all official documents, then spent three sessions debating what to call the committee that would enforce the ban.

(147 characters)

## Candidate 3

The bill to reduce paperwork passed unanimously, all 400 pages of it, with an appendix explaining how to request an exemption from reading it.

(142 characters)

## Candidate 4

They promised to drain the swamp, then commissioned a study on swamp drainage, then renewed the study's funding every year for a decade.

(136 characters)

## Candidate 5

Every speech begins with 'let me be clear,' followed by forty minutes of fog so thick the stenographer filed for hazard pay.

(124 characters)

## Candidate 6

They voted themselves a pay raise at 2 a.m., then held a press conference at noon about the importance of shared sacrifice.

(123 characters)

## Candidate 7

The hearing on government waste cost more than the program it was investigating, and the catering alone had its own line item and subcommittee.

(143 characters)

## Candidate 8

They outlawed honest mistakes in official reports, so now every error is described as an 'emerging opportunity for narrative adjustment.'

(137 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

The hypocrisy is concrete and visual: a law demanding plain-English explanations from algorithms, filed as a crooked photograph of a printout inside a filename-parody folder. It escalates in three beats (the law, the filing, the folder name) and every beat targets process rather than party, so the roast lands on lawmakers as a species.
